# Operating Partner Outreach

Outreach for the **operating-license model** (see `OPERATING_PARTNER_BRIEF.md`) — for
information, health-media, or reputation-focused dental-content companies that would
operate the asset, not for individual practices (those use `OUTREACH_DRAFTS.md`).

Use plain text. Lead with the asset as an opportunity, not a job. The framing below is
deliberately reputation-first: it self-selects operators who care about building a
trusted reference and filters out lead-mill shops.

Do not promise traffic, rankings, or revenue in any message.

---

## One-Page Asset Opportunity Sheet

*(This is the leave-behind you send when an operator asks for detail. One page, plain.)*

**dentalsurgerynewyork.com — Operating Partner Opportunity**

**What it is:** An independent, governed oral surgery reference for New York City patients on an exact-match domain — 97 pages across procedures, costs, boroughs, and patient questions, built on a route registry, an evidence-integrity system, cost-data separation, and a validation pipeline that rejects quality failures before publish. It is a reference asset, not a clinic site or a lead funnel.

**The opportunity:** The owner retains full ownership and licenses **operation and commercialization** to one qualified operator, in exchange for a share of the media revenue the operator generates. The owner supplies a turnkey, governed, monetization-ready asset; the operator brings distribution, relationships, and day-to-day operation.

**How it earns (permitted models only):** fixed-fee partner-practice placements, borough and procedure sponsorships, educational-guide underwriting (with visible disclosure), and aggregated demand-intelligence reports. **Not** pay-per-lead, not fee-splitting, not programmatic ads, not "best practice" rankings. Editorial independence and visible disclosure are non-negotiable and contractual.

**Why it's rare:** an exact-match, high-intent domain is uncommon on its own; combined with a governed page system, an evidence posture, and a ready commercialization framework, it is a reference an operator can build on rather than build from scratch — and one that stays clean and defensible for the operator's own reputation.

**What we're looking for in an operator:**
- A publisher / content mindset (sponsorship and placement), not a lead-generation shop.
- Medical-writing or clinical-review capability (a named licensed reviewer strengthens both credibility and search trust).
- Comfort operating an editorially independent asset — no routing its value to in-house clients.
- Openness to a **license + revenue-share** structure, not only a fixed retainer.

**What this is not:** not a sale of the domain, not a marketing-services retainer, not a referral network.

**Next step:** a short call, then a written operating/license agreement. The operator carries all US/NY operational compliance and indemnifies the owner; the owner's role is limited to licensing and receiving a royalty.
