## Overview

Medicaid coverage for oral surgery in New York depends on the specific procedure, the patient's enrollment category, and whether the procedure meets Medicaid's coverage criteria. New York is one of a relatively small number of states with broader adult dental Medicaid benefits, but coverage is not unlimited and varies significantly by procedure type.

This page provides an informational overview of how Medicaid dental benefits work in New York State. Individual coverage questions should be verified directly with the New York State Medicaid program or a provider who accepts Medicaid.

## Adult Dental Coverage Under New York Medicaid

New York State Medicaid covers a defined set of dental services for adult enrollees (ages 21 and older). Coverage categories include:

- emergency dental services
- diagnostic services (examinations, X-rays)
- preventive services (cleanings in certain circumstances)
- restorative services (fillings)
- oral surgery (tooth extractions, including surgical extractions)

Coverage for dental implants and elective cosmetic procedures is generally excluded under standard Medicaid dental benefits.

## Tooth Extractions

Tooth extractions — including surgical extractions of impacted teeth — are generally covered by New York Medicaid for enrolled adults when medically necessary. This includes:

- simple extractions
- surgical extractions, including impacted wisdom teeth, when the procedure is medically indicated

The provider must accept Medicaid and the procedure must meet Medicaid's criteria for medical necessity. Prior authorization may be required for certain procedures.

## Dental Implants

Dental implants are generally not covered by standard New York Medicaid adult dental benefits. Implants are classified as a prosthetic replacement that exceeds standard benefit coverage. Partial dentures and full dentures may be covered in certain circumstances as the covered tooth replacement option.

## Bone Grafting

Coverage for bone grafting under Medicaid depends on the indication. Grafting performed in connection with a covered surgical extraction may be reviewed for coverage, but elective bone grafting for implant preparation is generally not covered.

## Managed Care Plans

Many Medicaid enrollees in New York receive benefits through managed care plans (Medicaid HMOs) rather than through traditional fee-for-service Medicaid. Dental benefits under managed care plans may differ from standard Medicaid dental benefits. Enrollees should contact their specific plan to understand what oral surgery services are covered.

## How to Verify Coverage

- Contact New York State Medicaid directly or through the eMedNY system
- Contact the specific managed care plan if enrolled through a Medicaid HMO
- Ask the oral surgery provider's billing office whether they accept Medicaid and whether prior authorization is needed

## Questions to Ask Before Treatment

- Does this practice accept Medicaid for oral surgery?
- Is this procedure covered under my Medicaid benefit?
- Does this procedure require prior authorization?
- If I need bone grafting or other preparatory procedures, are those covered?
- What portion, if any, will I owe?
