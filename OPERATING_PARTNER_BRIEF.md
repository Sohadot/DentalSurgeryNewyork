# Operating Partner Brief

## What This Is

dentalsurgerynewyork.com is a governed New York oral surgery reference asset. This brief describes a single, specific arrangement: an **operating license**, not a sale and not a practice placement.

Under it, the owner retains full ownership of the asset — domain, codebase, content, governance system, and editorial independence — and licenses its **operation and commercialization** to a qualified operating partner in exchange for an ongoing share of the commercial revenue the operator generates from it.

This is a standard intellectual-property operating license. The counterparty is an **information, media, or relations company** — not a dental practice, and not a clinician. No arrangement in this brief involves any share of a clinical practice's treatment revenue.

---

## Who This Is For

The right operating partner is an information / relations / healthcare-media company that:

- can sell and service the asset's permitted commercial surfaces (fixed-fee placements, borough and procedure sponsorships, guide underwriting, demand-intelligence reports);
- already holds relationships with, or can reach, licensed New York oral surgery and dental implant practices;
- can operate a governed editorial asset without collapsing its independence into advertising;
- and — ideally — can supply a **named, New-York-licensed medical reviewer**, which strengthens the asset's expertise signals and its search-engine trust posture at the same time.

It is not for buyers seeking ownership (see `STRATEGIC_ACQUISITION_BRIEF.md`) or for individual practices seeking placement (see `PARTNER_BRIEF.md`).

---

## Why an Operating License (the logic)

The owner built a rare, high-craft asset: an exact-match, high-intent domain; 97 governed pages across procedures, costs, locations, and questions; a route registry; an evidence-integrity system; cost-data separation; and a ready-to-activate commercialization framework — all validated by a pipeline that rejects quality failures before build.

What the asset does not yet have is a sales-and-relationships engine. The operating partner brings exactly that. The owner supplies a turnkey, governed, monetization-ready asset; the operator supplies distribution, relationships, and day-to-day operation. The value of the asset's keywords and the quality of its construction are what justify the owner's ongoing royalty.

---

## What the Operating Partner Receives

- A turnkey, indexed, governed reference asset with an established page inventory and internal architecture.
- The right to generate revenue through the asset's **permitted commercial models** (below), keeping the agreed majority share.
- Operational control of publishing cadence, partner sales, and reporting — **within** the governance boundaries that protect the asset's value.
- A validation pipeline and decision log that keep the asset clean and defensible for the partner's own reputation.

## What the Owner Retains

- Full ownership of the domain, codebase, content, and governance framework.
- An ongoing **royalty / revenue share** on commercial revenue the asset generates.
- The right to audit governance adherence and to require correction of any breach.
- Clean **reversion** of the asset — uncompromised, with no undisclosed relationships — at the end of the term (consistent with the Future Sale Protection posture in `COMMERCIALIZATION_POSTURE.md`).

---

## Permitted Revenue the Operator May Generate

Bound by `COMMERCIALIZATION_POSTURE.md`. The operator may monetize **only** through:

1. Fixed-fee partner practice placements
2. Borough sponsorships
3. Procedure sponsorships
4. Educational guide underwriting (with visible "Underwritten by…" disclosure)
5. Monthly demand-intelligence reports (aggregated, anonymized, non-patient data)

**Expressly prohibited** (voids the license): pay-per-patient / pay-per-lead, revenue sharing or fee splitting with any practice, editorial control by any partner, undisclosed sponsored content, rankings sold as clinical superiority, intrusive ad formats, generic programmatic advertising (including AdSense) at this stage, and any collection of sensitive health information.

## Revenue Share (owner to finalize)

The owner sets the commercial terms; the following is a structure to complete, not a fixed offer:

| Term | Proposed shape — **[OWNER TO SET]** |
|------|--------------------------------------|
| Owner royalty | A fixed percentage of the operator's **net commercial revenue** from the asset (e.g., 20–40%) |
| Minimum guarantee | An optional monthly or annual minimum, so the asset is not left idle |
| Initial term | e.g., 12–24 months, with defined renewal |
| Performance floor | Minimum activity (e.g., partner seats sold, pages maintained) below which the owner may terminate |
| Exclusivity | Exclusive operation vs. non-exclusive — owner's choice |
| Reporting | Monthly revenue statement + governance-adherence confirmation |

Royalty is calculated on the **asset's media/commercial revenue only** — never on any practice's clinical revenue.

## Non-Negotiable Governance Conditions

The operating agreement must bind the operator to:

1. Editorial independence — no partner or the operator may alter, pre-approve, or suppress editorial content.
2. The full disclosure standard on every sponsored or underwritten surface.
3. The partner qualification baseline (valid NY license, no active NYSED disciplinary action, verifiable contact, written editorial-independence acknowledgment).
4. The prohibited-models list, in full.
5. No "recommended" or "best" clinical language, paid or unpaid.
6. Preservation of the validation pipeline and decision log.

Breach of any of these is a defined event of default with a cure period and reversion.

---

## Asset Protection

The arrangement is structured so the owner's asset is never diluted:

- No transfer of ownership.
- No undisclosed partner relationships created in the owner's name.
- No pay-per-patient liabilities inherited by the owner.
- Documented disclosure standards and a reproducible, clean program the owner can carry into any future sale or self-operation.

---

## How to Proceed

1. Mutual NDA (optional) before sharing operating specifics.
2. A written **Operating / License Agreement**, reviewed by a New York attorney (the medical-commercial and IP-licensing nature warrants it), executed before any operation begins.
3. Governance adherence confirmed in the agreement, not assumed.

No operating access, publishing rights, or partner sales occur before agreement execution.
