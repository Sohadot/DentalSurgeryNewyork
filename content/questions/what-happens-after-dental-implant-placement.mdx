## Introduction

Dental implant placement is one stage in a multi-phase treatment process. Understanding what happens in the months after the implant post is placed — including the osseointegration period, follow-up appointments, and what comes before the final crown — helps New York patients plan accurately and know what to expect.

---

## Immediately After Placement: The First 48–72 Hours

Following implant placement, the surgical site begins the initial healing response. Patients can expect:

- **Swelling and bruising** — Peaking at 48–72 hours, then gradually resolving over the following week. Ice application in the first 24 hours reduces peak swelling.
- **Pain and tenderness** — Managed with prescribed or OTC pain medication. Severe or worsening pain after day 3–4 should prompt contact with the provider.
- **Minor bleeding** — Expected in the first several hours. Persistent or heavy bleeding beyond the first day warrants a call to the provider.
- **Dietary restrictions** — Soft diet is required initially. Avoid anything that could disturb the surgical site or place direct load on the implant.
- **Oral hygiene** — The implant site requires gentle care. Avoid vigorous rinsing or disturbing the area. Providers typically prescribe a chlorhexidine rinse and provide specific cleaning guidance.

---

## The Osseointegration Period

After initial healing, the implant post undergoes osseointegration — the process by which the jawbone integrates with the implant surface. During this period:

- The implant is not loaded — no crown or prosthetic is attached yet
- A healing abutment or cover screw protects the site
- Timeline is typically 2–4 months in the lower jaw and 4–6 months in the upper jaw, varying by bone density, graft status, and implant type
- Patients return for periodic check-ins to assess healing

For background on this process: [What Is Osseointegration and Why Does It Matter?](/questions/what-is-osseointegration-and-why-does-it-matter)

---

## Stability Assessment

At the end of the osseointegration period, the provider assesses whether the implant has integrated successfully. This may involve:

- Clinical stability testing — the implant should feel completely solid with no movement
- Resonance frequency analysis in some practices — a non-invasive measurement of implant stability
- Imaging review if clinically indicated

If integration is confirmed, the case proceeds to the restorative phase. If the implant shows signs of mobility, the provider will evaluate whether to allow more healing time or remove and reassess.

---

## The Restorative Phase: Abutment and Crown

Once osseointegration is confirmed:

1. **Abutment placement** — The connector piece between the implant post and the crown. In some cases this is placed at the time of implant surgery; in others it is a separate procedure.
2. **Impressions or digital scan** — A precise record of the bite and surrounding teeth for crown fabrication.
3. **Temporary crown** — In some cases a temporary crown is placed while the permanent one is fabricated.
4. **Final crown delivery** — The permanent crown is placed and bite is verified and adjusted.

The restorative phase may be handled by the same oral surgeon or — commonly in New York — by a separate restorative dentist or prosthodontist working in coordination with the surgeon. Confirm the handoff arrangement before starting treatment.

---

## Total Treatment Timeline

| Stage | Typical Duration |
|-------|------------------|
| Initial post-operative healing | 1–2 weeks |
| Osseointegration | 2–6 months (varies by site and bone) |
| Stability assessment | 1 appointment |
| Crown fabrication and delivery | 2–4 weeks |
| **Total: implant placement to crown** | **3–8 months** |

Cases involving bone grafting before implant placement add additional time before this timeline begins.

---

## Questions to Ask Before Placement

- Who handles the restorative phase — you, or a separate dentist? How will the handoff work?
- How will you assess osseointegration, and what is the protocol if it does not proceed as expected?
- What diet and activity restrictions apply during the osseointegration period?
- What symptoms should prompt me to contact you before my scheduled follow-up?
