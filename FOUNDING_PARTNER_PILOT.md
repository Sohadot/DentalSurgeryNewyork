# Founding Partner Pilot — Internal Operating Note

## Purpose

This document defines the structure, intake process, and governance rules for the dentalsurgerynewyork.com Founding Partner Pilot. It is an internal reference for managing the pilot and ensuring compliance with `COMMERCIALIZATION_POSTURE.md` throughout.

---

## Pilot Objectives

1. Validate that the fixed-fee partner placement model produces stable revenue without editorial compromise.
2. Establish a reference cohort of early partners for reporting, renewal conversations, and program refinement.
3. Confirm that the disclosure and labeling framework is operationally manageable at small scale.
4. Generate initial recurring revenue to offset ongoing content and site operating costs.

---

## Pilot Structure

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Duration | 90 days from first partner onboarding date |
| Maximum seats | 5 practices (soft cap; expand only after governance review) |
| Pricing — monthly | $500 / month |
| Pricing — prepaid | $1,200 for full 90-day period (saves $300 vs. monthly) |
| Minimum term | 90 days (no mid-pilot cancellation) |
| Post-pilot renewal | Month-to-month at standard program rate, or renegotiated |

---

## Included Placement Surfaces

Each Founding Partner receives visible placement on:

1. **Partner directory** — Listed on `/partners` (existing public page)
2. **Relevant procedure pages** — Pages matching the practice's primary procedure focus
   - Implant-focused practice: `/procedures/dental-implants`, `/procedures/bone-grafting`
   - Extraction/general oral surgery: `/procedures/tooth-extraction`, `/procedures/wisdom-tooth-removal`
   - Sedation focus: `/procedures/oral-surgery-sedation`
   - Emergency care: `/procedures/emergency-oral-surgery`
3. **Relevant cost guide pages** — Cost pages matching the same procedure focus
4. **Borough page** — Where the practice's primary borough matches site location content (e.g., `/locations/manhattan`)

**Not included:** home page, methodology, core policy pages (`/disclaimer`, `/accuracy-policy`, etc.), question pages, or pages where placement would not be contextually coherent.

---

## Disclosure and Labeling

Every placement carries the following label, always visible and adjacent to the placement unit — not reducible to a footer-only or footnote disclosure:

> "Partner practice — sponsored placement. Inclusion does not constitute a clinical recommendation."

This wording is fixed. It may not be softened, removed, or replaced at a partner's request.

---

## Reporting

Monthly simple visibility report delivered by the 5th of the following month, covering:

- Confirmation of active placement pages during the period
- Any page additions or removals during the period
- No patient data, referral counts, treatment conversions, or click-level analytics are available or provided

The report is informational only. It is not a performance guarantee and does not constitute evidence of referral activity.

---

## Intake Process

1. **Outreach or inquiry received** — Use templates in `OUTREACH_DRAFTS.md`
2. **Qualification check** — Run against `PARTNER_TARGET_CRITERIA.md`; confirm NYSED license status at https://www.nysed.gov/professions
3. **Share PARTNER_BRIEF.md** — Send to the practice decision-maker
4. **Execute Partner Agreement** — Signed agreement required before any listing appears. The agreement must include:
   - Fixed-fee acknowledgment (no patient volume guarantee, no lead delivery)
   - Editorial independence clause (no content review, approval, or modification rights)
   - Disclosure consent (partner acknowledges the label will appear on all placements)
   - 90-day minimum term with no mid-pilot cancellation
5. **Collect payment** — Prepaid or first monthly fee before placement goes live
6. **Activate placement** — Add partner unit to relevant pages; confirm disclosure label is present and correctly worded
7. **Send confirmation** — List the specific pages where placement is live
8. **Month 1 report** — Deliver by Day 35

---

## Governance Rules

- No placement goes live before the Partner Agreement is signed and payment is received.
- No editorial content may be modified in response to a partner request, ever.
- Any request by a partner to alter, suppress, or soften editorial content triggers automatic disqualification and removal of placement within 5 business days. No refund is issued for the current period.
- If a partner is found to be subject to active NYSED disciplinary action after listing, placement is removed within 5 business days and not restored until the proceeding is resolved.
- Partner identity is disclosed to site visitors via the label — anonymous placements are not permitted.
- Placement units are added to the site's codebase as explicit, version-controlled content — not injected by third-party ad scripts.

---

## Pilot Review (Day 75)

Two weeks before pilot close, review each partner relationship:

- Any governance challenges? Any requests to alter content?
- Interest in renewal at post-pilot rate?
- Any issues with the disclosure framework in practice?
- Should the seat cap be raised for the post-pilot program?

Pilot outcome and lessons are documented in `DECISION_LOG.md` at close.

---

## Revenue Recognition Note

Fixed-fee income received under this pilot is unrelated to patient referrals, treatment completions, or clinical outcomes. It is placement advertising revenue. Tax, legal, and accounting treatment should be confirmed with the operator's advisors before the pilot begins.
