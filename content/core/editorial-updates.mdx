## About This Log

This page records content additions, structural changes, and governance decisions made on dentalsurgerynewyork.com. It exists to demonstrate that the site is actively maintained and to provide transparent documentation of how the editorial inventory has developed.

This is not a medical literature review log. No clinical reviewer is listed on this site unless one is engaged. Entries reflect site content decisions, not clinical evidence reviews. The informational framework used to produce content is described in [Methodology](/methodology) and [Accuracy Policy](/accuracy-policy).

---

## 2026-06-20 — Framework Intelligence Layer Completion and Question Library Depth

**Added (category framework pages — 3 new):**

- `/category-language` — The definitional vocabulary layer governing how key oral surgery terms are used across this reference: clinical terms, cost and billing terms, provider credential distinctions, insurance terminology, and New York-specific context. Defines terms such as OMFS, implant, osseointegration, estimate, treatment episode, predetermination, and directional range with the precision necessary for patient comparison and decision-making.
- `/protocol` — A structured 7-phase patient navigation framework covering the full oral surgery journey from initial assessment through multi-phase treatment sequences, with decision checkpoints and minimum documentation standards at each phase: recognition and initial assessment, specialist consultation, pre-authorization, pre-operative preparation, day of procedure, recovery and follow-up, and subsequent treatment.
- `/decision-compass` — A structured self-triage reference with 5 decision paths: implant candidacy assessment, bone graft need evaluation, sedation level selection, emergency recognition and triage, and second opinion evaluation. Each path ends at a specific action point — a question or documentation request to bring to a provider consultation.

**Added (question pages — 6 new):** `/questions/what-is-the-difference-between-a-periodontist-and-an-oral-surgeon`, `/questions/what-is-osseointegration-and-why-does-it-matter`, `/questions/can-i-get-dental-implants-if-i-have-gum-disease`, `/questions/what-causes-dry-socket-and-how-is-it-treated`, `/questions/when-should-i-see-an-oral-surgeon-instead-of-a-dentist`, `/questions/what-happens-after-dental-implant-placement`.

**Route index:** ROUTE_INDEX contains 56 entries. Question library expanded from 13 to 19 pages. Framework pages for Layers 2, 5, and 6 now have dedicated content alongside the Layer 3 Ontology added in the prior sprint.

**Agent-readable index:** `public/llms.txt` updated to reflect all 56 indexed routes including the expanded Category Framework section.

**Purpose:** Close the three most immediately executable framework gaps — Category Language (definitional authority), Protocol (patient journey governance), and Decision Compass (structured self-triage tool). Extend the question library depth with clinically specific topics: specialist scope comparison, osseointegration mechanics, gum disease as an implant candidacy barrier, dry socket risk and management, provider selection scope, and the post-implant treatment timeline. These additions collectively deepen the site’s position as the primary independent reference for oral surgery decision-making in New York.

## Update Standards

Content on dentalsurgerynewyork.com is updated when:

- New procedures, cost guides, or decision resources are added to the editorial inventory
- Pricing context or structural information becomes materially outdated
- A governance or policy change requires documentation
- An error is identified and corrected

This log is updated with each material content change. It does not include minor copy edits or formatting adjustments.

No entry on this log constitutes a medical literature review. Content reflects editorial research, publicly available information, and the informational framework defined in [Methodology](/methodology) and [Accuracy Policy](/accuracy-policy).
