## Purpose and Limitations

This page provides a consolidated, directional overview of cost ranges for oral surgery procedures in New York. It is not a price list, not a quote, and not a treatment recommendation. It consolidates directional context from the site's individual cost guides into a single reference.

All ranges on this page reflect publicly available information and common pricing structures in New York City oral surgery. Actual costs depend on case complexity, provider pricing, sedation requirements, imaging, insurance coverage, and patient-specific factors. Patients should request written, itemized estimates from licensed providers before making any treatment decision. The figures below are directional context only — not a price quote from any practice.

---

## Tooth Extraction

Tooth extraction costs vary by whether the procedure is simple or surgical.

**Simple extraction** (erupted tooth, local anesthesia): approximately $150–$400 per tooth.

**Surgical extraction** (impacted, fractured, or complex root): approximately $300–$800 or more per tooth.

**Additional cost factors often billed separately:**
- Imaging (dental X-rays, CBCT): $50–$400
- Sedation if applicable: $100–$600 or more
- Socket preservation graft if placed at time of extraction: $300–$800 per site

For full context, see [Tooth Extraction Cost in New York](/costs/tooth-extraction) and the [Tooth Extraction Procedure Guide](/procedures/tooth-extraction).

## Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom tooth removal costs depend on how many teeth are removed, their impaction depth, and sedation requirements.

**Single tooth, simple extraction**: approximately $200–$400.

**Single tooth, surgical (impacted)**: approximately $350–$700 or more.

**Full set (four teeth) under IV sedation**: total estimates in New York frequently range from $1,500–$3,500 depending on complexity.

For full context, see [Wisdom Tooth Removal Cost in NYC](/costs/wisdom-tooth-removal) and the [Wisdom Tooth Removal Procedure Guide](/procedures/wisdom-tooth-removal).

## Dental Implants

Dental implant pricing in New York is frequently quoted as a per-implant figure that excludes essential components. A complete single-tooth implant typically involves three billable elements: the implant post, the abutment, and the crown.

**Implant post (fixture)**: approximately $1,500–$3,000.

**Abutment**: approximately $300–$700.

**Crown**: approximately $1,000–$2,000.

**Complete single-tooth implant (all components)**: approximately $3,000–$6,000 or more in New York City, excluding imaging and bone grafting if required.

For full context, see [Dental Implant Cost in New York](/costs/dental-implants) and the [Dental Implants Procedure Guide](/procedures/dental-implants).

## Bone Grafting

Bone grafting costs vary significantly by procedure type.

**Socket preservation graft** (placed at time of extraction): approximately $300–$800 per site.

**Ridge augmentation**: approximately $800–$2,500 or more.

**Sinus lift** (lateral window approach): approximately $1,500–$4,000 or more per side.

Material choice (allograft, xenograft, synthetic) and membrane use affect cost. Bone grafting is frequently billed separately from the implant procedure and should appear as a distinct line item in any written estimate.

For full context, see [Bone Grafting Cost in New York](/costs/bone-grafting) and the [Bone Grafting Procedure Guide](/procedures/bone-grafting).

## Oral Surgery Sedation

Sedation fees are typically billed separately from the surgical procedure fee.

**Nitrous oxide**: approximately $50–$150 per appointment.

**Oral (enteral) sedation**: approximately $150–$300.

**IV moderate sedation**: approximately $200–$600 or more depending on procedure duration.

**IV deep sedation or general anesthesia**: varies significantly and may be billed by time increment.

Patients should confirm that the provider holds the appropriate NYSED Anesthesia Permit for the proposed sedation level before agreeing to treatment.

For full context, see [Oral Surgery Sedation Cost in New York](/costs/oral-surgery-sedation) and the [Oral Surgery Sedation Procedure Guide](/procedures/oral-surgery-sedation).

## Emergency Oral Surgery

Emergency oral surgery visits typically carry urgency premiums above standard procedure fees.

**Urgent appointment premium**: approximately $100–$300 above standard appointment fees for same-day or next-day emergency access.

**Emergency extraction including exam and imaging**: approximately $500–$1,500 or more.

**Emergency imaging if not included**: approximately $100–$400.

Patients should ask whether the quoted emergency fee includes examination, imaging, and any post-operative medication, or whether these are billed separately.

For full context, see [Emergency Oral Surgery Cost in New York](/costs/emergency-oral-surgery) and the [Emergency Oral Surgery Procedure Guide](/procedures/emergency-oral-surgery).

## Factors That Apply Across All Estimates

Several cost factors consistently appear across oral surgery procedures and are frequently excluded from initial quotes:

- **Imaging** — CBCT scans can add $200–$400 and are commonly excluded from base quotes
- **Sedation** — Almost always billed separately regardless of procedure
- **Separate anesthesia provider** — If a CRNA or anesthesiologist is present, their fee is additional
- **Post-operative visits** — May be included or billed per visit
- **Lab fees** — Crowns, custom abutments, and surgical guides involve separate lab costs
- **Insurance gap** — Dental insurance frequently covers a fraction of oral surgery costs; benefit maximums and waiting periods are common

To compare estimates meaningfully, request written itemized estimates from at least two licensed providers. Each estimate should specify every procedure code, material, sedation level, and imaging requirement.
