## Introduction

Bone grafting cost in New York varies significantly based on the type of procedure involved. Socket preservation at the time of extraction, ridge augmentation for an area with established bone loss, and sinus lift procedures are not interchangeable — they represent different levels of clinical complexity, procedure time, and materials.

## Market Context Snapshot

Socket preservation in New York is often quoted in a range of roughly **$300 to $800** per site. Ridge augmentation is directionally higher, frequently quoted in a range of roughly **$800 to $2,500 or more** depending on complexity. Sinus lift procedures are frequently quoted in a range of roughly **$1,500 to $4,000** or more per side depending on the approach and volume required. These figures are directional context — they do not represent any specific provider’s pricing.

## Procedure Types and Cost Structure

**Socket preservation (ridge preservation):** The most commonly encountered graft procedure, typically performed at the time of tooth extraction. It involves placing graft material into the extraction socket to reduce bone resorption. It is generally the least complex and least expensive graft procedure.

**Ridge augmentation:** A more involved procedure to restore bone volume in areas where significant resorption has already occurred. Typically performed as a standalone procedure and requires a healing period before implant placement.

**Sinus lift (sinus augmentation):** Used when the sinus floor in the upper jaw has expanded into the planned implant site. Requires specific training and additional procedure time. Lateral window sinus lifts — for larger volume additions — are generally more expensive than transcrestal approaches used for more limited cases.

Each procedure type has different complexity, recovery timelines, and cost implications.

## What Changes the Total Cost

Beyond graft type, cost in New York may vary based on:

- **Graft material** — Autograft (bone from the patient’s own body) requires a second surgical site and typically adds cost. Allograft, xenograft, and synthetic materials vary in price and clinical rationale.
- **Membrane use** — A collagen or other barrier membrane placed over the graft site is billed separately in many cases.
- **Imaging** — CBCT scans are commonly used to plan grafting cases and may be billed separately.
- **Sedation** — Particularly for sinus lifts and ridge augmentation, IV sedation is commonly recommended and adds to total cost.
- **Borough and practice model** — Practice overhead varies across New York City boroughs.

## Bone Grafting and Implant Planning

Bone grafting cost should generally be considered in the context of total implant treatment cost. Socket preservation at extraction may reduce the complexity of future implant placement. Failing to preserve the socket can result in additional grafting requirements later that are more expensive and more involved than early intervention.

Patients considering implants should ask, at the time of extraction, whether socket preservation is recommended for their case — and what it adds to the cost of the extraction visit.

## What to Clarify Before Comparing Estimates

Before comparing providers, patients should request clarity on:

- which type of graft procedure is being proposed
- what graft material will be used and where it comes from
- whether a membrane is included in the estimate
- whether imaging is included
- whether sedation is included and at what level
- what the expected timeline is between grafting and implant placement

A written itemized estimate makes these components visible.
