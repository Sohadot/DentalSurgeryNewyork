## Introduction

Comparing oral surgery quotes in New York is more difficult than comparing prices for most other services. Two quotes for the same procedure may reflect very different scopes of treatment, which is why a lower number does not always mean a lower total cost.

## Why Quotes Are Difficult to Compare Directly

Oral surgery quotes often vary because:

- different providers include different components in their base price
- imaging, sedation, and follow-up visits may or may not be itemized
- promotional pricing may highlight an entry-level cost rather than the full treatment cost
- quotes may cover different stages of a multi-stage procedure

Without understanding what each quote covers, comparison is unreliable.

## What a Complete Estimate Should Address

A thorough written estimate for oral surgery should make clear:

- the procedure or procedures being quoted
- whether imaging and x-rays are included or billed separately
- whether sedation or anesthesia is included and at what level
- whether extraction, if needed, is part of the cost
- whether bone grafting or socket preservation is included or separate
- whether follow-up appointments are covered
- what happens if additional work is discovered during treatment

## What Low Quotes Often Omit

Promotional quotes for procedures like dental implants sometimes present only the surgical component cost, not the full restoration cost. For implants, the quoted number may exclude the abutment, the final crown, or both — leaving a significant cost gap visible only after the patient has committed to treatment.

For other procedures, imaging and sedation are common items that appear as separate line charges after the fact.

## What to Ask Each Provider

Before accepting any quote as a basis for comparison:

- Can you provide an itemized written estimate?
- What is and is not included in the total shown?
- Are there circumstances under which additional costs would apply?
- How firm is this estimate once treatment begins?
- Is the sedation level recommended for my case included in this price?

## How to Make the Comparison Meaningful

Once itemized estimates are available, compare what is covered rather than the totals alone. An estimate that includes sedation, imaging, and follow-up care represents a different value than one that does not, even if the headline number appears lower.
