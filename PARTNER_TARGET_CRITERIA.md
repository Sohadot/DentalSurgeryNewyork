# Partner Target Criteria

## Purpose

This document defines the qualification criteria for identifying and prioritizing practices for Founding Partner Pilot outreach. It is an internal reference only. No practice should receive outreach, be listed in the partner directory, or be offered placement unless it meets the baseline criteria below.

---

## Baseline Qualification (All Required)

All of the following must be confirmed before a practice receives outreach:

1. **Valid New York State license** — The lead provider holds a current NYSED license in:
   - Oral and maxillofacial surgery (preferred), or
   - Periodontology with documented implant and/or bone grafting practice, or
   - General dentistry with documented, verifiable oral surgery specialization

   License status is confirmed at: https://www.nysed.gov/professions

2. **No active disciplinary action** — NYSED Office of the Professions shows no pending or active disciplinary proceeding, consent order, or license restriction at the time of outreach.

3. **Verifiable public presence** — The practice has:
   - A functioning website with current contact information
   - A verifiable physical address within New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, or Staten Island)
   - A working phone number or contact form

4. **Procedure alignment** — The practice's primary services align with at least one of the site's current content areas:
   - Dental implants
   - Wisdom tooth removal or tooth extraction
   - Bone grafting (socket preservation, ridge augmentation, sinus lift)
   - Oral surgery sedation
   - Emergency oral surgery

5. **No apparent incompatibility with editorial independence** — Nothing in the practice's public communications, review history, or prior interactions suggests they would require content modification as a condition of any commercial relationship.

---

## Preferred Characteristics (Not Required, but Weighted in Prioritization)

Practices with the following characteristics are better candidates for early outreach:

- **Independent or small group practice** (1–5 providers) — More likely to engage directly; less likely to require lengthy corporate procurement
- **Manhattan or Brooklyn primary location** — Highest overlap with current site traffic and content depth
- **Implant and bone grafting focus** — Highest-value content alignment with existing procedure and cost surfaces
- **Active web presence** — Functioning website, visible Google Business Profile, public reviews — confirms the practice is actively seeking patients
- **Transparent pricing language** — Practices that already communicate cost ranges publicly are aligned with the site's posture and less likely to object to cost guide content

---

## Disqualifying Factors

Do not contact or list practices that:

- Are under active NYSED disciplinary review, suspension, or consent order
- Operate exclusively outside New York City without a verified NYC practice location
- Are owned by a national DSO or corporate dental chain where individual placement decisions require corporate procurement approval (misaligned incentive structure; slows program)
- Have publicly documented pending malpractice issues that would create reputational risk for the site
- Have previously requested content changes, paid placement disguised as editorial content, or indicated they expect ranking elevation in exchange for payment
- Operate primarily as cosmetic dental practices without verifiable oral surgery scope

---

## Priority Tiers for First Outreach

### Tier 1 — Highest Priority
- Board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons in Manhattan or Brooklyn
- Independent practice (not DSO-owned)
- Primary focus: dental implants, bone grafting, or full-scope oral surgery
- Active, professional web presence

### Tier 2 — Strong Candidates
- Periodontists with documented implant and bone grafting focus in any NYC borough
- Oral surgeons in Queens, Bronx, or Staten Island (borough diversity value)
- Small group practices (2–3 providers) with aligned procedure focus

### Tier 3 — Consider After Pilot Seats Are Filled
- General dentists with oral surgery specialization (scope harder to verify)
- Practices outside Manhattan/Brooklyn with limited content overlap
- Any practice where the decision-maker is not the clinical provider

---

## Outreach Volume Expectations

The pilot is capped at 5 practices. To fill 5 seats with qualified partners, expect to contact 20–30 practices across Tier 1 and Tier 2. Not every practice will respond; not every interested practice will pass full qualification review.

Track each contact in a simple log (date contacted, tier, response, outcome). Do not re-contact practices that have said no without a material change in circumstances.

---

## Qualification Checklist (Complete Before Each Outreach)

- [ ] NYSED license confirmed active
- [ ] No active disciplinary action on NYSED records
- [ ] NYC address and website verified
- [ ] Procedure focus aligns with at least one site content area
- [ ] No disqualifying factors present
- [ ] Assigned to Tier 1 or Tier 2
- [ ] Not previously contacted and declined
