# Commercialization Posture

## Purpose

This document defines the governing principles for commercial activity on dentalsurgerynewyork.com. It exists to protect the editorial independence of the asset while enabling structured, transparent revenue mechanisms that do not compromise its value as an independent oral surgery reference.

This document is binding on all commercial decisions, partner agreements, and revenue structures applied to this asset.

---

## What This Asset Is

dentalsurgerynewyork.com is an independent oral surgery guidance platform for patients in New York City. Its value rests on editorial independence, informational accuracy, and the absence of undisclosed commercial influence on patient-facing content.

Commercial activity is permitted only within the boundaries defined here.

---

## Permitted Commercial Models

### 1. Fixed-Fee Partner Practice Placements

Licensed oral surgery and dental implant practices may purchase visible placement within the site's partner directory, procedure pages, or borough guides. Placement is sold at a fixed monthly fee unrelated to patient volume, treatment cost, or clinical outcomes.

### 2. Borough Sponsorships

A single practice may sponsor a borough section (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, or Staten Island), gaining designated visible placement within borough-specific pages. Sponsorship does not alter editorial content on those pages.

### 3. Procedure Sponsorships

A single practice may sponsor a procedure section, gaining designated visible placement within those procedure pages. Sponsorship does not alter editorial content.

### 4. Educational Guide Underwriting

A qualified partner practice may underwrite a specific educational guide, cost-index page, or patient checklist resource published on the site. Underwriting is a fixed-fee commercial model with full editorial independence preserved.

The underwriting practice's name appears on the page as "Underwritten by [Practice Name]." A visible disclosure accompanies every underwritten page:

> "This page is underwritten by [Practice Name]. Editorial content is independently produced and is not subject to the underwriter's review or approval."

**What underwriting cannot influence:**
- Editorial conclusions on any page
- Directional cost ranges, cost structures, or pricing context
- Safety guidance, warnings, or risk disclosures
- Provider selection standards or criteria
- Comparisons between procedure options or approaches
- Any content on pages outside the specific underwritten resource

Underwriting is available to Reference Partner and Category Sponsor tier practices. Available surfaces include procedure guides, cost guides, the cost index, and consultation checklists.

### 5. Monthly Demand Intelligence Reports

Aggregated, anonymized search demand data compiled from public signals may be packaged and sold to licensed practices as market intelligence. No individually identifiable patient data is included.

---

## Prohibited Commercial Models

- **Pay-per-patient / pay-per-lead** — No revenue structure that charges per patient referred, per consultation booked, or per treatment completed.
- **Revenue sharing or fee splitting** — No percentage of a practice's clinical revenue in exchange for placement or referral. This may constitute illegal fee splitting under New York Education Law and ADA ethics guidelines.
- **Editorial control by partners** — No partner may alter, review in advance, or suppress any editorial content on the site.
- **Undisclosed sponsored content** — All sponsored placements and underwritten content must carry clear visitor-facing disclosure.
- **Rankings sold as advertising** — No practice may be positioned as clinically superior solely on the basis of payment.
- **Intrusive ad units** — No popups, interstitials, or display advertising formats that interrupt the guidance experience.
- **Generic programmatic advertising** — Google AdSense and similar networks are not permitted at this stage of asset development.
- **Collection of sensitive health information** — The site does not collect medical histories, diagnoses, or treatment details that could create HIPAA obligations.
- **Illegal referral payments** — Any payment structure constituting illegal fee splitting or kickback under New York Education Law or applicable federal law is prohibited regardless of framing.

---

## Editorial Independence

Commercial relationships do not influence:

- Procedure guide content
- Cost guide content
- Decision guide content
- Location guide content
- Methodology or accuracy policy
- Question-and-answer content
- Warnings, caveats, or risk disclosures
- Cost ranges, directional pricing, or cost factor descriptions
- Safety guidance or emergency triage guidance
- Provider selection standards or criteria

Sponsored or partner-placed content is always separated from editorial content and clearly labeled.

---

## Partner Visibility Rules

- Partner placement does not constitute a clinical recommendation or endorsement.
- The site does not verify clinical outcomes, patient satisfaction, or practice quality beyond confirming New York State licensure status.
- Partner status does not mean “best,” “recommended,” or “preferred” in any clinical sense.
- A partner practice is responsible for its own clinical care, pricing, scheduling, and patient communication.
- Partner placement is available to licensed oral surgeons and dental implant providers in New York only.

---

## Disclosure Standard

Every sponsored placement must include a visible disclosure — legible, present on every relevant page, not hidden in footnotes:

> "Partner practice — sponsored placement. Inclusion does not constitute a clinical recommendation."

For underwritten guides:

> "This page is underwritten by [Practice Name]. Editorial content is independently produced and is not subject to the underwriter's review or approval."

---

## No Medical Recommendation Policy

dentalsurgerynewyork.com does not recommend specific practitioners, diagnose conditions, suggest treatment, or advise patients on clinical decisions. This applies equally to paid and unpaid content. A partner practice may not be described as “recommended” in the clinical sense.

---

## Partner Qualification Baseline

Practices eligible for placement must:

1. Hold a valid New York State license in oral and maxillofacial surgery, periodontology, or general dentistry with documented oral surgery practice.
2. Not be subject to active disciplinary action by the NYSED Office of the Professions at time of listing.
3. Have a functioning website and verifiable contact information.
4. Agree in writing that the site's editorial content is not subject to their review, approval, or modification.

---

## Future Sale Protection

This posture protects the asset's acquisition value. Any future buyer inherits:

- No undisclosed partner relationships
- No pay-per-patient liabilities
- No editorial compromise
- Documented disclosure standards
- A reproducible, clean partner program structure
