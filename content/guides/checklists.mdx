## Introduction

These checklists are designed for patients preparing for an oral surgery consultation, treatment estimate review, or procedure in New York. They are not clinical protocols and do not substitute for guidance from a licensed provider.

Use these as a starting point for conversations with your oral surgeon — not as a self-diagnosis or treatment decision framework.

---

## Tooth Extraction Consultation Checklist

Before proceeding with a tooth extraction in New York:

- Is a written, itemized estimate provided before the procedure date?
- Is imaging (X-ray or CBCT) included in the quoted fee, or billed separately?
- Is this a simple or surgical extraction? Has the provider explained the difference for your specific tooth?
- If sedation is offered or recommended, is the sedation fee itemized separately?
- What, if anything, follows the extraction? (Socket preservation graft? Implant planning?)
- What is the post-operative protocol? (Dietary restrictions, activity limits, medication, follow-up visit?)
- Is dry socket treatment or a complication visit included, or billed separately?
- Has the provider reviewed relevant medical history, including anticoagulants, bisphosphonates, diabetes, or prior jaw radiation?

---

## Wisdom Tooth Removal Checklist

Before wisdom tooth removal in New York:

- Is the current status of each tooth confirmed by imaging?
- Is the degree of impaction explained? (Soft tissue, partial bony, full bony)
- Are all four teeth being removed in one session, or staged? What is the rationale?
- Is the quoted fee for all teeth, or per tooth?
- Is sedation recommended? What type? Is the provider's sedation credential confirmed?
- Is imaging included in the fee or billed separately?
- What are the specific post-operative care instructions?
- What is the expected recovery timeline for your specific case?
- What are the warning signs to call the office about? (Dry socket, infection signs?)
- Is a follow-up visit included or billed separately?

## Dental Implant Planning Checklist

Before committing to a dental implant in New York:

- Does the written estimate include all components? (Implant post, abutment, crown)
- Is a CBCT scan included, or billed separately?
- Is bone grafting required? Is that cost itemized separately?
- Is sedation included, or an additional cost?
- What is the total treatment timeline from extraction to final restoration?
- Who performs each phase? Are the surgeon and restorative dentist different providers with separate fees?
- What are the risks specific to the proposed placement site?
- What is the implant brand and model?
- What is the protocol if osseointegration does not occur?
- Has insurance coverage been confirmed in writing for each phase?

---

## Bone Grafting Checklist

Before proceeding with a bone graft in New York:

- What type of graft is proposed? (Socket preservation, ridge augmentation, sinus lift)
- What graft material is being used? (Autograft, allograft, xenograft, synthetic) What are the trade-offs?
- Is a membrane being placed? Is that cost included in the quoted fee?
- What is the healing timeline before an implant can be placed?
- Is the grafting billed separately from the extraction or implant?
- What is the provider's training and experience with this specific graft type?
- What are the risks of graft failure, and what happens if the graft does not integrate?
- Will follow-up imaging confirm successful integration before implant placement?

## Sedation Safety Questions

Before accepting sedation beyond nitrous oxide for oral surgery:

- What level of sedation is being proposed? (Minimal / Moderate / Deep / General Anesthesia)
- Does the provider hold a valid NYSED Anesthesia Permit for the proposed sedation level?
- Who administers sedation — the surgeon, a CRNA, or a separate anesthesiologist?
- Who monitors the patient during the procedure?
- What monitoring equipment is in use? (Pulse oximetry, blood pressure, capnography?)
- What is the emergency protocol if a sedation complication occurs?
- Have all medications, supplements, and medical history been reviewed for sedation contraindications?
- What are the pre-procedure fasting requirements?
- Is a responsible adult required to escort the patient post-procedure?
- What are the specific post-sedation care instructions?

## Emergency Call Preparation Checklist

If you are calling an oral surgeon for an emergency appointment in New York:

- Note when pain or swelling started and how it has changed
- Note whether you have fever, difficulty swallowing, or difficulty breathing — if yes, go to the ER, not the dental office
- Have your current medications list available
- Have your dental insurance information available
- Have the name and number of any prior treating dentist available
- Be prepared to describe: location of pain, severity (1–10), what makes it better or worse
- Ask the office: Can you be seen today? What is the cost of the emergency visit? Is imaging included?
- If the office cannot see you, ask for a referral to an oral surgeon who handles emergency cases

## Cost Estimate Clarification Checklist

When reviewing any cost estimate for oral surgery in New York:

- Is the estimate in writing and itemized by procedure?
- Does the estimate include imaging?
- Does the estimate include sedation, or is it quoted separately?
- Does the estimate specify the type and amount of anesthesia?
- For implants: does the estimate include the implant, abutment, and crown?
- For bone grafting: are the graft material, membrane, and any imaging included?
- What is explicitly excluded? Ask directly: "What is not in this estimate?"
- Has the office confirmed insurance coverage for each procedure code in writing?
- Is there a payment plan? What are the financing terms?
- What triggers an additional charge after the procedure begins?
