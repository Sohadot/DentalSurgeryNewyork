# Partner Practice Brief

## What This Site Is

dentalsurgerynewyork.com is an independent oral surgery guidance platform for patients in New York City. It publishes procedure guides, cost guides, location guides, and decision support content without advertiser influence. The site does not accept pay-per-patient arrangements, does not sell clinical rankings, and does not promise patient volume.

Its value to patients — and to partners — depends on maintaining that independence.

---

## The Partner Program

Licensed oral surgery and dental implant practices in New York may participate in the Partner Practice Program, which provides transparent, disclosed placement on relevant pages of the site.

Placement is:

- **Fixed-fee** — not tied to patient volume, referrals, or treatment revenue
- **Disclosed** — clearly labeled on every page where it appears
- **Non-editorial** — placement does not alter guidance content on the site
- **Non-exclusive** per category, except where Category Sponsorship is selected

---

## Founding Partner Pilot

A limited number of practices are being offered the opportunity to participate in the site's inaugural 90-day Founding Partner Pilot before broader program launch.

**Pilot terms:**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Duration | 90 days |
| Placement fee | $500 / month, or $1,200 prepaid for the full 90-day period |
| Seats | Limited (first-come, by qualification) |
| Surfaces | Partner directory, relevant procedure pages, relevant cost guide pages |
| Report | Monthly simple visibility report (placement confirmation and page list) |
| Post-pilot | Month-to-month renewal or renegotiated at standard program rate |

**What the pilot does not include:**

- Patient referrals or lead delivery of any kind
- Clinical endorsement or "recommended" designation
- Ranking above other practices based on payment
- Influence over editorial content, framing, or disclosures on any page
- Patient contact information or inquiry routing

---

## Disclosure Language

Every placement carries the following label, visible to site visitors:

> "Partner practice — sponsored placement. Inclusion does not constitute a clinical recommendation."

This label appears adjacent to each placement unit. It is not reducible to a footnote or footer-only disclosure.

---

## Qualification Requirements

To be eligible for partner placement, a practice must:

1. Hold a valid New York State license in oral and maxillofacial surgery, periodontology, or general dentistry with a documented oral surgery focus.
2. Not be subject to active disciplinary action by the NYSED Office of the Professions at the time of listing.
3. Have a functioning website and verifiable contact information.
4. Agree in writing that the site's editorial content is not subject to the practice's review, approval, or modification.

---

## No Medical Recommendation

dentalsurgerynewyork.com does not clinically recommend any practice. Partner status means a practice has agreed to transparent, disclosed placement terms — nothing more. The site does not verify clinical outcomes, patient satisfaction scores, or any measure of clinical quality beyond confirming New York State licensure status.

---

## How to Proceed

Express interest by contacting the address listed on the site's `/partners` page. A signed Partner Agreement is required before any placement appears. No public listing is created before agreement execution and payment collection.
