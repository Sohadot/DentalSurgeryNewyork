## Purpose

This page summarizes the governance architecture behind DentalSurgeryNewYork.com. It exists to make the reference system inspectable without turning the site into a sales page.

The site is not presented as a clinical authority, a provider ranking engine, or an insurance approval service. It is an independently governed patient reference focused on oral surgery decisions in New York.

## What Is Governed

The asset uses automated validation gates to reduce common failure modes in medical-adjacent content:

- every public page must be registered in the central route index
- content files cannot exist outside route governance
- internal links are checked against known routes
- title and description lengths are validated
- draft filler language is rejected
- high-risk absolute claims are rejected
- cost pages must preserve directional, non-quote framing
- insurance and Medicaid pages must preserve verification and prior-approval language
- evidence sources have review cadences

These controls do not make the site perfect. They make quality failures more visible before publication.

## Evidence Integrity

Sensitive topics are mapped to evidence profiles. Pages involving cost, Medicaid, insurance, emergency care, sedation, recovery, implants, or clinical risk receive stronger validation than ordinary informational pages.

The evidence registry currently includes:

- New York State Medicaid dental provider information
- the current eMedNY dental policy and procedure manual
- Medicare dental services coverage guidance
- internal methodology, accuracy, disclaimer, cost-data, and coverage-verification standards

Official sources are reviewed on a shorter cadence than internal editorial standards because policy and coverage information changes more frequently than site methodology.

## Cost and Coverage Boundaries

Cost guidance is directional context. It is not a quote, fee schedule, or promise of market pricing. Coverage guidance is plan-dependent and must be verified with the insurer, Medicaid plan, or provider billing office before treatment.

This distinction is central to the site's trust model: useful information without pretending to replace individual verification.

## Commercial Boundary

The site may support disclosed fixed-fee partner visibility or underwriting, but payment does not control editorial content, clinical recommendations, or provider ranking.

The commercial model is designed to preserve the reference function first. If commercial use would weaken editorial independence, the governance posture rejects it.

## What This Page Does Not Claim

This page does not claim:

- clinical review by a licensed clinician
- legal or insurance advice
- guaranteed search performance
- guaranteed traffic or buyer interest
- clinical endorsement of any provider
- complete coverage of all New York oral surgery information

It documents the controls currently operating around the asset.
